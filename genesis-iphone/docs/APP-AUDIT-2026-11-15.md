# Genesis app audit — November 15, 2026

Scope: native Genesis Controller on iPhone and iPad, Mac/provider switching, and the separate Genesis G2 connection. Native version: **0.2.1 (19)**. No Genesis Mini firmware change is included.

## Native repairs

- Switching between Claude and Codex immediately replaces the previous connection label with **Connecting…** or **Pair this Mac**.
- A blocked chat tap now explains the actual condition: open the agent app, unlock the Mac, allow Accessibility, install the app, wait for the connection, or pair the Mac. Unsupported-feature wording remains for genuinely unavailable chat controls.
- Routing, draft persistence, dictation, Send, and Follow ownership behavior are unchanged. Changing Mac or provider still releases Follow; enable it again and open a chat to establish the new display target.

## Separate G2 restart recovery

Genesis G2 **0.4.1** recovered its connection to **both Minis** after actual Even restarts across three new loopback origins. Each Mac retained one enrolled installation, accepted the renewed origins, and returned successful requests without adding those new origins to its static allowlist. This establishes restart-origin recovery for the tested paired installation; it does not establish unlimited background execution or locked-phone reliability.

## Mac companion repair

Companion **0.5.1-preview** is installed and running on both Minis with the same signed executable. The Apple development identity and bundle ID are unchanged. Each Mac retains its previous pairing identity and profiles, and the prior app is saved locally as a rollback.

Claude navigation now retries fresh accessibility reads when a snapshot is transiently missing or incomplete. It uses bounded deadlines and rejects ambiguous fork/title matches, process changes, and stale context. Confirmation repeats observations, never chat clicks or Send/Stop actions. Exhausting the read budget reports a verification failure rather than claiming the chat is unsupported.

- Full Swift typecheck and the mapper regression script passed. Independent review passed all **17** targeted navigation checks.
- Live companion `openChat` checks completed **C1 → C2 → C1 on both Minis**, with all six receipts confirming the exact requested target. Mini 1 took 1.19–1.74 seconds per navigation; Mini 2 took 2.00–2.97 seconds in this small check, not a performance benchmark.
- Both native bridges reported Claude and Codex ready after installation. No test prompt, Send, Stop, or permission approval was issued.
- These checks exercised the installed companion API. They do not replace the pending physical build-19 UI acceptance.
